The 1,000 hp Advanced Combat Engine (ACE), developed by Cummins in partnership with the U.S. Army, will be showcased at Australia’s Land Forces Exposition in Melbourne from September 11-13. Incorporating advanced technologies not currently available in diesel combat engines, the compact four-cylinder ACE is designed for infantry fighting vehicles and main battle tanks.
